Chompbot

Move Chompbot around the grid and eat every number that fits the rule. Eat a wrong one and you lose a life, but you'll see the math that shows why. Watch out for glitches that wander in from the edges and scramble empty squares. Clear all 8 levels to win. Keyboard: arrow keys or WASD to move, Space to eat, P to pause. You can also tap a square to step toward it, and tap your own square to eat.
